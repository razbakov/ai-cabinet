# Year in Review

This skill runs a comprehensive life assessment using the 12 Apostles Life Wheel framework. You score 12 apostle-virtues on a scale of 1-10 (total out of 120), compare with previous assessments to spot trends, and create an actionable plan for the spokes that need the most attention.

The 12 Apostles Life Wheel ensures you're not just optimizing for work while other areas of life deteriorate. It's the big-picture check that keeps everything in balance.

## How It Works

1. **Score each spoke** — Rate 12 apostle-virtues from 1-10: Peter (Faith & Courage), John (Love & Relationships), James the Greater (Purpose & Mission), Andrew (Service & Helping Others), Philip (Learning & Wisdom), Bartholomew (Authenticity & Integrity), Thomas (Truth & Discernment), Matthew (Work & Stewardship), James the Less (Humility), Jude Thaddeus (Hope & Perseverance), Simon the Zealot (Passion & Commitment), and Matthias (Readiness & Obedience).
2. **Compare trends** — If previous assessments exist, the agent shows how each spoke has changed over time. Rising spokes get acknowledged. Declining spokes get flagged.
3. **Identify patterns** — The agent looks for correlations: are spokes linked? Does work and stewardship come at the cost of love and relationships? Does purpose track with passion and commitment?
4. **Select focus spokes** — Choose 2-3 spokes to focus on in the coming quarter. The agent helps you pick based on impact (which improvements cascade into other spokes) rather than urgency.
5. **Create action plan** — For each focus area, define one concrete habit, one measurable goal, and one check-in cadence. Simple and sustainable beats ambitious and abandoned.

## When to Use

- Quarterly (every 3 months) as your life-level strategic review
- At the start of a new year for annual planning
- After a major life change (new job, move, relationship change) to recalibrate
- When you feel successful on paper but empty inside — the assessment reveals which areas are being neglected

## Requirements

- No integrations required — this is a reflective exercise
- Previous assessment files provide trend data (optional but powerful)
- 30-45 minutes of honest reflection time
- A quiet environment where you can think clearly without rushing
